    How to play

    Build words together

    1. Create your own room or join a room with at least one other player.
    2. Players take turns choosing a letter for everyone.
    3. Place each letter on your 5x5 board.
    4. When every board reaches 24/25 letters, each player chooses their own final letter.
    5. Make words across or down to score points.

    Scoring: 3-letter words score 3 points, 4-letter words score 4 points, and 5-letter words score 10 points.
